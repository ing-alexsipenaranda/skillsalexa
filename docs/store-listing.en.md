# Distribution Listing — English

**Public name:** Evoll AI

**Short description:** Access your Evoll tasks, schedule, and finances with Alexa.

**Description:**

Evoll AI connects Alexa to your Evoll account so you can ask about tasks,
reminders, schedules, budgets, spending, goals, and debts. You can request a
daily summary, continue a conversation, ask for more details, and create tasks
or reminders after explicit confirmation.

The Evoll app controls which modules Alexa may access. Private mode limits
sensitive information and suppresses visual cards. A linked Evoll account is
required.

**Examples:**

- “Alexa, open Evoll assistant.”
- “Alexa, ask Evoll assistant what my tasks are.”
- “Alexa, ask Evoll assistant how my budgets are doing.”
- “Alexa, ask Evoll assistant what I have today.”

**Keywords:** productivity, tasks, schedule, finance, budget, goals.

**Suggested category:** Productivity.

**Requirements:** Evoll account, account linking, and a supported language.
